About

I study information science and am interested in AI, automation, product development, community building, and the relationship between technology and society.

In my undergraduate research, I worked on GAN-based game level generation. Beyond research, I care about how AI and automation can be applied to real communities, local regions, and everyday systems.

I am interested not only in technology itself, but in how it can change people's lives, communities, and the time humans can spend on meaningful things.
